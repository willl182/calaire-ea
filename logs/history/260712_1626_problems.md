# Problem Record (Troubleshooting)

**Severity**: Medium

## The Problem

The first synthesis over-constrained the service to a four-gas core product, deferred O3, and used the reference value regardless of participant count. It also treated the approximately EUR 1,600 benchmark too cautiously and initially proposed technical artifacts already covered by the QMS.

## User-Requested Corrections

- Permit one, several or all five gases, including O3.
- Use the reference value below 12 technically valid results and robust participant consensus at 12 or more.
- Remove a1-a7 grading.
- Retain approximately EUR 1,600 as the working five-gas price anchor.
- Focus the artifact blueprint on commercial service artifacts, not technical round artifacts.
- Include analyzer/generation-equipment spares, maintenance and consumables.
- Compare multi-component certified mixture versus individual cylinders.
- Model current configuration-specific capacity.

## The Solution / Workaround

The definitive proposal and blueprint were corrected to preserve these rules. The QMS repository was reviewed and mapped so commercial artifacts reference existing PSEA controls. Cost and capacity sections were expanded with explicit decision and verification requirements.

## Learnings to Preserve

- Do not replace explicit service requirements with recommendations from comparative analysis.
- Keep technical readiness gates without turning selectable O3 into a deferred commercial component.
- Participant thresholds apply to technically valid results per gas/level.
- Commercial viability must use physical analyzer-position capacity, not generic market assumptions.
- Audit existing QMS assets before proposing new operational artifacts.
