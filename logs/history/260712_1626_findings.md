# Technical Finding / Milestone

**Context**: CALAIRE-EA commercial PT service design

## The Discovery

The PSEA QMS already covers the technical round lifecycle in substantial detail. The MVP gap is the commercial service shell: catalogue, scheduled offer, price model, expression of interest, quotation, commercial registration fields, terms, order review, enrollment/revenue tracking, confirmation, commercial changes, report delivery, feedback and renewal.

The commercial model must use configuration-specific capacity rather than a generic participant assumption. Current planning limits are four participants/analyzers for an individual gas and three participants/six analyzers for simultaneous CO/SO2.

Previously omitted lifecycle costs are material: spares, maintenance and consumables for the CALAIRE O3, SO2, CO and NOx analyzers, dynamic calibrator and zero-air generator. Certified-gas procurement also requires an explicit multi-component-mixture versus individual-cylinder or hybrid decision.
