# Technical Finding / Milestone

**Context**: CALAIRE-EA Commercial Service Artifacts — MVP Skeleton Implementation

## The Discovery

The MVP blueprint (`ptservice_art_prop.md`) defines 15 commercial artifacts (CS-01 to CS-15) structured in 6 folders. All artifacts can be bootstrapped as Markdown templates with [FILL] placeholders, enabling parallel work streams once CS-01 (commercial decision sheet) is approved.

Key design decisions preserved:
- Commercial layer references QMS documents under `docs/qms/` rather than duplicating technical clauses.
- Pricing architecture: `common fee + gas fees + additional analyzer fees`.
- Capacity limits hard-coded: 4 analyzers (individual gas), 3 participants / 6 analyzers (simultaneous CO/SO₂).
- Mandatory wording controls prevent a1–a7 grading, accreditation claims, and pilot/paid confusion.
