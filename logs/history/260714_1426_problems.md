# Problem Record: Stale Commercial and QMS Assumptions

**Severity**: High

## The Problem

Commercial templates still encoded five separately priced gases and per-analyzer
fees after management selected a flat four-block offer. P-PSEA-17 was also named
as the complaints procedure while containing the retired audit placeholder.

## User-Requested Corrections

- Use Universidad Nacional de Colombia as legal provider and CALAIRE as executor.
- Treat NO/NO₂ as one NOx block.
- Use a flat proposal fee for one to four blocks, not analyzer-based billing.
- Restore and use the correct complaints procedure.

## The Solution / Workaround

CS-01, CS-04, CS-06, CS-08, CS-10, market templates and the blueprint were
aligned. P-PSEA-17 was restored and complaint responsibilities were assigned.

## Learnings to Preserve

- Do not reopen frozen proposal decisions without revising CS-01.
- Do not treat proposal approval as price, legal or market-release approval.
- Validate named files by their internal title and purpose, not filename alone.
