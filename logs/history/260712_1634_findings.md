# Technical Finding / Milestone

**Context**: CALAIRE-EA QMS consolidation and commercial-service interface

## The Discovery

The active QMS has been consolidated under `docs/qms/`. Proposals and commercial artifacts require an explicit precedence rule so archived duplicates and analyses cannot be mistaken for controlled requirements.

The navigation map can remain a concise technical map while making this boundary visible. Its detail panel now reports canonical `docs/qms/01_bloque_general` folders instead of linking to archived summary-card pages, and round evidence is represented under `docs/qms/EA-<codigo-ronda>`.
