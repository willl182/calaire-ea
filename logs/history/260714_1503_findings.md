# Milestone: CS-10 Operational Workbook Implemented

**Context**: CALAIRE-EA commercial release gates

## The Discovery

CS-10 can be implemented without inventing finance, legal or technical evidence.
The workbook separates user inputs from calculated controls and applies the
approved CS-01 capacity and wait-list rules. A separate synthetic copy validates
the tracker portion of the workflow without placing test records in the clean
institutional template.

The implementation includes controlled lists, calculated analyzer totals,
payment balances and percentages, wait-list grouping/ranking, last-slot and
over-capacity warnings, payment-deadline warnings, stale-EoI checks and the five
required operational views. Formula and dashboard sheets are protected. The
initial password must be changed and the files must be placed in protected UNAL
storage before real use; workbook protection alone is not an information-access
control.

The synthetic workbook was opened and recalculated through LibreOffice. Core
totals, partial-payment calculations, exception counts and sheet protection were
verified. Both XLSX containers pass archive-integrity checks and `git diff
--check` passes.
