# Milestone: Commercial Proposal Decisions Frozen

**Context**: CALAIRE-EA commercial service artifacts

## The Discovery

The international reference is a bundled UBA gaseous-air PT price, not a fee
per gas or per analyzer. The approved proposal therefore uses four commercial
blocks and a flat indicative COP fee per participating organization. Technical
capacity is still consumed by analyzer and block, but it does not drive the
customer price during the proposal stage.

CS-01 v0.3 records the settled decisions. Detailed rules and affected artifacts
are referenced rather than duplicated here.
