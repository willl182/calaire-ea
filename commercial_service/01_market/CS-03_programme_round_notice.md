# CS-03 — Annual Programme and Round Notice

**Status:** PLANNED  
**Owner:** Round coordinator  
**Required before:** Marketing release  
**Last revision:** 2026-07-14 (added notice version, draft/final SLA distinction)  
**QMS code reference:** ver `00_control/qms_code_equivalence.md`.

## Purpose

Convert the generic service into a purchasable scheduled round.

## Notice version control

Each notice issued for a round carries a version number. Any change in date, capacity, gases, fees or status after first publication requires a new version (v1, v2, …) and a short change note attached to the public announcement. The first publication is v1.

Location: [FACILITY], Medellín, Colombia
Dates: [START] to [END]
Gases offered: [LIST]
Capacity: [LIMITS]
Registration: [OPEN] to [DEADLINE]

## Controls

- Does not duplicate the detailed PSEA technical instructions.
- Prices and capacity match CS-01 and CS-04.
- Dates align with existing PSEA planning documents.
